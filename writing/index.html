About this page

No schedule, no categories

There is no editorial calendar here, and no attempt to keep a cadence. A note gets written when something built turns out to be worth explaining — which so far means about four notes per project, in one burst, and then silence until the next thing ships.

All four are about Local Route Launcher, because it is the only thing finished enough to have decisions worth recording. When another project reaches that point, its notes will join this list rather than getting a section of their own.
